# Islam Makhachev — Lightweight Champion & #1 Pound-for-Pound Fighter

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Islam Makhachev
- **Nickname**: (none official)
- **Born**: October 27, 1991, Makhachkala, Dagestan, Russia
- **Nationality**: Russian (Dagestani)
- **Height**: 5 ft 10 in (178 cm)
- **Reach**: 70.5 inches (179 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Sambo, Wrestling, Judo, Submission Grappling

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Islam Makhachev has an MMA record of 26 wins and 1 loss.

- **Wins**: 6 by knockout/TKO, 12 by submission, 8 by decision
- **Losses**: 1 (early career loss to Adriano Martins in 2015)

## Championship History

**UFC Lightweight Championship (155 lbs)**: Won on October 22, 2022 at UFC 280, defeating Charles Oliveira by submission (rear-naked choke) in round 2 in Abu Dhabi.

Title Defenses:
1. **UFC 284 (2023)**: Defeated Alexander Volkanovski (P4P #1 at the time) by unanimous decision in Perth, Australia, in what many called the best lightweight title fight ever.
2. **UFC 294 (2023)**: Defeated Alexander Volkanovski by KO in round 1 in the immediate rematch — a dominant statement.
3. **UFC 302 (2024)**: Defeated Dustin Poirier by submission in round 5.

## Khabib Nurmagomedov Connection

Islam Makhachev is the protégé and closest training partner of retired champion Khabib Nurmagomedov. The two have trained together since childhood in Dagestan. Khabib has cornered every one of Makhachev's UFC fights and has explicitly stated that Makhachev will surpass his own legacy. The Dagestani fighting system — bred in the mountains and refined at AKA — has produced back-to-back lightweight champions.

## Pound-for-Pound Rankings

Following his two wins over Alexander Volkanovski and his dominant title defense against Poirier, Makhachev was widely ranked as the #1 pound-for-pound fighter in the world by ESPN, UFC, and major MMA outlets, taking the mantle from Jon Jones.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

- **Grappling Dominance**: Like Khabib, Makhachev's wrestling and sambo base makes him nearly impossible to keep standing. He has 100% takedown defense in recent UFC fights.
- **Submission Depth**: His submission game is considered even more diverse than Khabib's, finishing opponents with arm-triangles, rear-naked chokes, and neck cranks.
- **Striking Evolution**: Has dramatically improved his striking, adding a powerful jab and counter right hand that now threatens elite strikers.
- **Pace**: Maintains elite pace for all 25 minutes in championship rounds.

## UFC 280 — Title Capture vs. Charles Oliveira

UFC 280 in Abu Dhabi is considered one of the greatest UFC events ever produced. Makhachev and Oliveira — two of the best grapplers in lightweight history — fought for the vacant title after Oliveira was stripped for missing weight. Makhachev secured the win with a dominant second-round rear-naked choke finish.

## Legacy

Islam Makhachev is being positioned as the fighter to dominate the lightweight division for the next decade. His style — technically superior Dagestani grappling combined with improved striking — represents the evolution of the Khabib system. At 32 years old, he has his best years ahead of him.
