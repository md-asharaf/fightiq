# Alex Pereira — Light Heavyweight Champion

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Alex Gomes Pereira
- **Nickname**: Poatan (meaning "Fists of Stone" in Tupi-Guaraní)
- **Born**: July 7, 1987, São Paulo, Brazil
- **Nationality**: Brazilian
- **Height**: 6 ft 4 in (193 cm)
- **Reach**: 79 inches (201 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Kickboxing, Muay Thai, Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Alex Pereira has an MMA record of 10 wins and 2 losses.

- **Wins**: 7 by knockout/TKO, 1 by submission, 2 by decision
- **Losses**: 2 (both to Israel Adesanya — one TKO, one KO)

## Championship History

1. **UFC Middleweight Championship (185 lbs)**: Won on November 12, 2022 at UFC 281, defeating Israel Adesanya by TKO in round 5.

2. **UFC Light Heavyweight Championship (205 lbs)**: Won on November 11, 2023 at UFC 295, defeating Jiří Procházka by TKO in round 2 to claim his second UFC title.

Title Defenses at Light Heavyweight:
1. **UFC 300 (2024)**: Defended against Jiří Procházka by KO in round 1.
2. **UFC 307 (2024)**: Defended against Khalil Rountree Jr.

## Kickboxing Career

Before entering MMA, Alex Pereira was a multi-time World Kickboxing Champion:
- **GLORY Middleweight Kickboxing Champion**
- **GLORY Super Middleweight Kickboxing Champion**
- **WGP Kickboxing Middleweight World Champion**

His kickboxing record is approximately 34 wins and 7 losses. He is best known for knocking out Israel Adesanya twice in kickboxing (2016 and 2017), including a brutal left hook knockout that remains one of the most devastating finishes in kickboxing history.

## Notable MMA Fights

- **UFC 268 (2021)**: MMA debut — defeated Andreas Michailidis by TKO in round 1.
- **UFC 276 (2022)**: Defeated Sean Strickland by KO in round 1, moving into the title picture.
- **UFC 281 (2022)**: Shocked the world by TKO-ing champion Israel Adesanya in round 5 after being hurt earlier in the fight.
- **UFC 287 (2023)**: Lost to Adesanya by KO in round 2, losing the middleweight title.
- **UFC 295 (2023)**: Moved to light heavyweight and stopped Jiří Procházka by TKO in round 2.
- **UFC 300 (2024)**: Destroyed Procházka by KO in round 1.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

- **Devastating KO Power**: Pereira possesses one of the most extraordinary knockout punches in combat sports history. His left hook is particularly feared.
- **Elite Kickboxing Foundation**: 10+ years of elite international kickboxing competition at the highest levels.
- **Pressure Fighter**: Relentlessly attacks, backing opponents into corners and creating finishing opportunities.
- **Surprising Durability**: Despite being dropped in fights (vs. Adesanya, vs. Procházka), he has shown an ability to recover and finish.
- **Grappling Development**: Has been adding wrestling and jiu-jitsu rapidly to complement his striking.

## Legacy

In under three years, Alex Pereira went from MMA newcomer to two-division UFC champion, becoming one of the most feared finishers in the history of the sport. His unique kickboxing pedigree and his rivalry with Adesanya make him one of the most compelling athletes in modern UFC.
