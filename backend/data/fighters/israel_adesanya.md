# Israel Adesanya — Middleweight Champion

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Israel Mobolaji Temitayo Adesanya
- **Nickname**: The Last Stylebender
- **Born**: July 22, 1989, Lagos, Nigeria (raised in New Zealand)
- **Nationality**: Nigerian-New Zealander
- **Height**: 6 ft 4 in (193 cm)
- **Reach**: 80 inches (203 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Kickboxing, Muay Thai, Taekwondo

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Israel Adesanya has an MMA record of 24 wins and 3 losses.

- **Wins**: 15 by knockout/TKO, 1 by submission, 8 by decision
- **Losses**: 3 (Jan Blachowicz, Alex Pereira twice)

## Championship History

**UFC Middleweight Championship (185 lbs)**: Won on October 5, 2019 at UFC 243, defeating Robert Whittaker by KO in round 2 in Melbourne, Australia. Defended the title five times before losing to Alex Pereira.

## Notable Fights

- **UFC 234 (2019)**: Defeated Anderson Silva by unanimous decision — a generational torch-passing moment.
- **UFC 236 (2019)**: Defeated Kelvin Gastelum by unanimous decision in an all-time great performance over 5 rounds (interim title).
- **UFC 243 (2019)**: Knocked out Robert Whittaker in round 2 in front of 57,000 fans in Melbourne.
- **UFC 248 (2020)**: Won split decision over Yoel Romero in a controversial tactical fight.
- **UFC 253 (2020)**: Submitted Paulo Costa in round 2 with a rear-naked choke (first and only UFC submission finish).
- **UFC 259 (2021)**: Lost to Jan Blachowicz by unanimous decision in a failed light heavyweight title bid.
- **UFC 263 (2021)**: Defeated Marvin Vettori by unanimous decision.
- **UFC 276 (2022)**: Defeated Jared Cannonier by unanimous decision.
- **UFC 281 (2022)**: Lost to Alex Pereira by TKO in round 5 (championship rounds), losing the title.
- **UFC 287 (2023)**: Defeated Alex Pereira by KO in round 2, reclaiming the title.
- **UFC 305 (2024)**: Lost to Dricus du Plessis by submission in round 4.

## Kickboxing Background

Before entering MMA, Adesanya had a remarkable kickboxing career with a record of approximately 80-5, fighting in Glory Kickboxing and other major promotions. His striking pedigree is among the most decorated of any UFC champion. This background gives him a 15-year head start in striking development compared to most MMA fighters.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

- **Range Control**: Uses his 80-inch reach and footwork to fight at the absolute end of his range, making him nearly untouchable.
- **Counter-Striking**: Masterful at baiting opponents into attacking and punishing them with precision counters.
- **Jab**: One of the best jabs in MMA, used both offensively and as a measurement tool.
- **Kicks**: Diverse kicking arsenal including round kicks, front kicks, oblique kicks, and spinning techniques.
- **Creativity**: Known for showboating and unorthodox setups that confuse opponents.
- **Weakness**: Grappling has been exploited by elite wrestlers (Blachowicz) and power strikers (Pereira).

## Rivalry with Alex Pereira

The Adesanya-Pereira rivalry is one of the most dramatic in combat sports. Their history began in kickboxing, where Pereira knocked Adesanya out in a 2017 Glory Kickboxing match. In MMA, Pereira stopped Adesanya by TKO at UFC 281. Adesanya got revenge at UFC 287, stopping Pereira by KO in round 2. The series stands at 2-2 across kickboxing and MMA.

## Legacy

Israel Adesanya is widely regarded as one of the most technically gifted strikers in MMA history. His five title defenses, his win over Anderson Silva, and his global fanbase make him one of the most significant champions of his era.
