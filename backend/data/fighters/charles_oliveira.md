# Charles Oliveira — Lightweight Champion & Record-Holder

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Charles do Bronx Oliveira
- **Nickname**: Do Bronx
- **Born**: October 17, 1989, Guarujá, São Paulo, Brazil
- **Nationality**: Brazilian
- **Height**: 5 ft 10 in (178 cm)
- **Reach**: 74 inches (188 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, Boxing, Wrestling

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Charles Oliveira has an MMA record of 34 wins and 9 losses.

- **Wins**: 9 by knockout/TKO, 21 by submission, 4 by decision
- **Losses**: 9 (mostly early career defeats)

## UFC Records

Charles Oliveira holds more UFC records than any other fighter:
- **Most Submission Wins in UFC History**: 21 (surpassed Royce Gracie's previous record)
- **Most Finishes in UFC History**: 19 finishes (combined KO+submission)
- **Most Performance Bonuses in UFC History**: 19 performance bonuses

## Championship History

**UFC Lightweight Championship (155 lbs)**: Won on May 15, 2021 at UFC 262, defeating Michael Chandler by TKO in round 2 after surviving a knockdown in round 1.

Title Defenses:
1. **UFC 269 (2021)**: Defeated Dustin Poirier by submission (rear-naked choke) in round 3.
2. **UFC 274 (2022)**: Was stripped of the title the day before the fight for missing weight. Defeated Justin Gaethje by submission in round 1. (Fought as challenger, won the belt but was denied due to weight miss)

He was then champion again after claiming the vacant title but lost it to Islam Makhachev at UFC 280.

## Notable Fights

- **UFC 262 (2021)**: Won the title in devastating fashion against Michael Chandler.
- **UFC 269 (2021)**: Submitted Dustin Poirier (a veteran of 30+ fights) in round 3 after surviving a rough early exchange.
- **UFC 274 (2022)**: Submitted Justin Gaethje in round 1 in one of the most exciting title fights ever — though he was denied the belt for missing weight.
- **UFC 280 (2022)**: Lost to Islam Makhachev by rear-naked choke in round 2 in a matchup of two elite grapplers.
- **UFC 300 (2024)**: Defeated Arman Tsarukyan in a five-round war.

## Early Career Struggles

Oliveira's career had a difficult start. He compiled a 3-4 UFC record through 2012-2013 and faced multiple losses. He was nearly cut from the roster. His perseverance through these lean years makes his eventual championship ascent one of the greatest comeback stories in MMA history.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

- **Submission Game**: Oliveira's BJJ is arguably the most dangerous in MMA history. He has finished world-class grapplers with an astonishing variety of techniques — rear-naked chokes, arm-bars, guillotines, anacondas, triangles, and more.
- **Chin**: Despite a reputation for durability issues, Oliveira has repeatedly survived adversity to come back and finish fights.
- **Cardio**: Elite conditioning allows him to hunt finishes well into championship rounds.
- **Constant Improvement**: Added meaningful boxing and wrestling skills each year.

## Legacy

Charles Oliveira is a living legend of Brazilian MMA in the tradition of Royce Gracie and Anderson Silva. His record-shattering submission numbers, his unlikely championship run, and his thrilling fighting style have made him a fan favorite worldwide.
