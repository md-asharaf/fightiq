# Jon Jones — Light Heavyweight & Heavyweight Champion

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Jonathan Dwight Jones
- **Nickname**: Bones
- **Born**: July 19, 1987, Rochester, New York, USA
- **Nationality**: American
- **Height**: 6 ft 4 in (193 cm)
- **Reach**: 84.5 inches (215 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Wrestling, Muay Thai, Boxing, Jiu-Jitsu

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Jon Jones has an MMA record of 27 wins, 1 loss (controversial disqualification), and 1 no contest. He is widely considered the greatest MMA fighter of all time (GOAT).

- **Wins**: 11 by knockout, 6 by submission, 10 by decision
- **Losses**: 1 (disqualification vs. Matt Hamill, 2009 — considered an anomaly)
- **No Contest**: 1 (vs. Daniel Cormier, overturned due to failed drug test)

## Championship History

Jon Jones is a two-division UFC champion:

1. **UFC Light Heavyweight Championship**: Jones first won the light heavyweight title on March 19, 2011, defeating Shogun Rua by TKO in round 3. He went on to defend the title 11 times — the most successful light heavyweight title reign in UFC history.

2. **UFC Heavyweight Championship**: On March 4, 2023, at UFC 285, Jones won the vacant UFC Heavyweight Championship by submitting Ciryl Gane in round 1, becoming only the third fighter in UFC history to win titles in two weight classes.

## Notable Fights

- **UFC 128 (2011)**: Defeated Mauricio "Shogun" Rua by TKO (R3) to become the youngest UFC champion ever at age 23.
- **UFC 135 (2011)**: Defeated Rampage Jackson by unanimous decision, defending his title for the first time.
- **UFC 140 (2011)**: Submitted Lyoto Machida with a standing guillotine choke in round 2.
- **UFC 145 (2012)**: Defeated Evans by unanimous decision in one of the most anticipated rematches in UFC history.
- **UFC 152 (2012)**: Submitted Vitor Belfort via arm-triangle choke, despite taking the fight on 8 days' notice.
- **UFC 159 (2013)**: Defeated Chael Sonnen by TKO in round 1 after Sonnen injured himself.
- **UFC 165 (2013)**: Won a split decision over Alexander Gustafsson in a fight widely regarded as one of the greatest in UFC history.
- **UFC 178 (2014)**: TKO'd Daniel Cormier in round 3 in a heated rivalry fight.
- **UFC 197 (2016)**: Defeated Ovince Saint Preux via unanimous decision (interim title).
- **UFC 214 (2017)**: Defeated Daniel Cormier by KO in round 3 — later overturned to NC due to failed drug test.
- **UFC 232 (2018)**: Defeated Alexander Gustafsson by TKO in round 3.
- **UFC 239 (2019)**: Defeated Thiago Santos by split decision in his last light heavyweight title defense.
- **UFC 285 (2023)**: Defeated Ciryl Gane by submission (rear-naked choke) in round 1, winning the Heavyweight Championship.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

Jon Jones is celebrated for his highly unorthodox and adaptable fighting style. His key attributes include:

- **Reach Advantage**: His 84.5-inch reach allows him to attack from distances no other fighter can match.
- **Oblique Kicks**: Jones popularized the lead-leg oblique kick aimed at the opponent's knee, drawing controversy but proving highly effective.
- **Ground-and-Pound**: Elite wrestling combined with creative elbows from top position.
- **Clinch Work**: Devastating elbows, trips, and dirty boxing in the clinch.
- **Fight IQ**: Arguably the best strategic mind in MMA, constantly adapting mid-fight.
- **Submission Defense**: Despite facing elite grapplers, Jones has never been submitted.

## Rivalry with Daniel Cormier

The rivalry between Jon Jones and Daniel Cormier is considered the greatest in MMA history. The two fought twice:
- **UFC 182 (2015)**: Jones won by unanimous decision in a grappling-heavy contest.
- **UFC 214 (2017)**: Jones KO'd Cormier in round 3, but the result was overturned to a NC after Jones tested positive for the steroid turinabol.

## Controversies and Suspensions

Jones' career has been marked by repeated anti-doping violations and personal conduct issues:

- **2012**: Arrested for DUI in Binghamton, NY.
- **2015**: Hit-and-run accident in Albuquerque, NM; suspended by NSAC.
- **2016**: Failed drug test (clomiphene/letrozole); suspended 1 year by USADA.
- **2017**: Failed drug test (turinabol metabolites); UFC 214 result overturned.
- **2021**: Arrested for DUI and firearm tampering in Las Vegas.

Despite controversies, his dominance inside the octagon remains unquestioned.

## Legacy

Jon Jones is frequently ranked as the #1 pound-for-pound MMA fighter of all time. His 11 light heavyweight title defenses is a divisional record. He holds wins over the most champions and former champions of any fighter in UFC history, including Shogun Rua, Rampage Jackson, Lyoto Machida, Rashad Evans, Vitor Belfort, Chael Sonnen, Glover Teixeira, Alexander Gustafsson (twice), Daniel Cormier (twice, one NC), Anthony Smith, Thiago Santos, Dominick Reyes, Ryan Spann, and Ciryl Gane.
