# Conor McGregor — Two-Division UFC Champion

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Conor Anthony McGregor
- **Nickname**: The Notorious
- **Born**: July 14, 1988, Dublin, Ireland
- **Nationality**: Irish
- **Height**: 5 ft 9 in (175 cm)
- **Reach**: 74 inches (188 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Southpaw Boxing, Karate, Kick-boxing

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Conor McGregor has an MMA record of 22 wins and 6 losses.

- **Wins**: 19 by knockout/TKO, 1 by submission, 2 by decision
- **Losses**: 4 by submission (3 by Khabib/Diaz/Poirier), 2 by TKO

## Championship History

Conor McGregor became the first fighter in UFC history to hold two divisional titles simultaneously:

1. **UFC Featherweight Championship (145 lbs)**: Won on December 12, 2015 at UFC 194, defeating José Aldo by KO in just 13 seconds — the fastest finish in UFC title fight history.

2. **UFC Lightweight Championship (155 lbs)**: Won on November 12, 2016 at UFC 205, defeating Eddie Alvarez by TKO in round 2, becoming the first UFC champion to hold belts in two weight classes at the same time.

## Notable Fights

- **UFC 194 (2015)**: Defeated José Aldo in 13 seconds — the fastest KO in UFC title fight history. Aldo had been undefeated for a decade.
- **UFC 196 (2016)**: Lost to Nate Diaz by rear-naked choke in round 2 at welterweight (170 lbs) — McGregor's first UFC loss.
- **UFC 202 (2016)**: Defeated Nate Diaz by majority decision in a five-round war, one of the most exciting rematches in UFC history.
- **UFC 205 (2016)**: Defeated Eddie Alvarez by TKO at Madison Square Garden, becoming the simultaneous two-division champion.
- **UFC 229 (2018)**: Lost to Khabib Nurmagomedov by rear-naked choke in round 4 in one of the most anticipated fights in MMA history. Post-fight brawl overshadowed the result.
- **UFC 246 (2020)**: Defeated Donald "Cowboy" Cerrone by TKO in 40 seconds — one of the fastest finishes in UFC history.
- **UFC 257 (2021)**: Lost to Dustin Poirier by TKO in round 2; McGregor's calf kick strategy was nullified.
- **UFC 264 (2021)**: Lost to Dustin Poirier by TKO in round 1 after breaking his tibia and fibula.

## Boxing Match vs. Floyd Mayweather Jr.

On August 26, 2017, McGregor faced undefeated boxing champion Floyd Mayweather Jr. in a professional boxing match. McGregor acquitted himself well through the early rounds but was stopped by TKO in round 10. The event generated over 4.3 million PPV buys in North America, making it one of the highest-grossing combat sports events ever.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

- **Left Hand Power**: McGregor's straight left hand (southpaw cross) is one of the most feared strikes in MMA.
- **Timing & Distance Management**: Expert at reading range and timing counter-punches.
- **Movement**: Fluid footwork with karate-influenced stance switching.
- **Pressure**: Psychologically dominant fighter who uses pre-fight trash talk and mental warfare to destabilize opponents.
- **Weakness**: Ground game and cardio have been exploited in losses.

## Business Ventures & Cultural Impact

Conor McGregor is one of the most financially successful athletes in combat sports history. In 2021, he sold a majority stake in his whiskey brand Proper No. Twelve to Proximo Spirits for reportedly $600 million. He was listed on Forbes' list of the world's highest-paid athletes multiple times. McGregor played the role of himself in the 2021 movie "Roadhouse."

## Legacy

McGregor transformed the business of MMA, consistently breaking PPV records. His fights against Khabib Nurmagomedov (2.4 million PPV), Nate Diaz (1.6 million PPV twice), and Donald Cerrone broke numerous records. He is credited with bringing mainstream pop culture attention to UFC and helping the organization expand internationally, particularly in Europe and Ireland.
