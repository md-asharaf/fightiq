# Stipe Miocic — Greatest UFC Heavyweight Champion of All Time

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Stipe Miocic
- **Nickname**: Stone Cold
- **Born**: August 19, 1982, Euclid, Ohio, USA
- **Nationality**: American (Croatian heritage)
- **Height**: 6 ft 4 in (193 cm)
- **Reach**: 80 inches (203 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Boxing, Wrestling, Muay Thai

## Fighting Record

As of 2024, Stipe Miocic has an MMA record of 20 wins and 4 losses.

- **Wins**: 12 by knockout/TKO, 1 by submission, 7 by decision
- **Losses**: 4 (Francis Ngannou twice, Daniel Cormier twice)

## Championship History

**UFC Heavyweight Championship (265 lbs+)**: Won on May 14, 2016 at UFC 198, defeating Fabricio Werdum by TKO in round 1 in Brazil — considered one of the biggest upsets in heavyweight history at the time.

Title Defenses:
1. **UFC 203 (2016)**: Defeated Alistair Overeem by TKO in round 1.
2. **UFC 209 (2017)**: Defeated Junior Dos Santos by TKO in round 3.
3. **UFC 220 (2018)**: Defeated Francis Ngannou by unanimous decision (Ngannou's first UFC loss).

Most Title Defenses: **3 consecutive UFC Heavyweight Championship defenses** — the most by any fighter in the division's history.

## Notable Fights

- **UFC 198 (2016)**: Won title in front of a hostile crowd in Brazil.
- **UFC 220 (2018)**: Defeated a primed Francis Ngannou by unanimous decision when many predicted a quick KO.
- **UFC 226 (2018)**: Lost the title to Daniel Cormier by KO in round 1 in an upset.
- **UFC 241 (2019)**: Rematch with Cormier — Miocic stopped Cormier by TKO in round 4, reclaiming the title.
- **UFC 252 (2020)**: Third fight with Cormier — Miocic won by unanimous decision to retain the title.
- **UFC 260 (2021)**: Lost the title to Francis Ngannou by brutal KO in round 2.
- **UFC 309 (2024)**: Rematch with Jon Jones for the Heavyweight Championship — lost by submission in round 3.

## Professional Firefighter

Stipe Miocic famously balanced his championship MMA career with his work as a volunteer firefighter in Oakwood, Ohio, and later as a full-time firefighter. He has responded to fire calls between training sessions and fights, embodying a blue-collar, working-class ethos that has made him one of the most respected athletes in combat sports.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

- **Boxing Fundamentals**: Elite boxing technique with powerful hooks and combinations.
- **Chin**: Extraordinary durability — has absorbed devastating shots and continued fighting.
- **Wrestling**: Strong wrestling base that allows him to mix takedowns with his striking effectively.
- **Cardio**: Elite cardio for a heavyweight — able to maintain pace for 5 rounds.
- **Fight IQ**: Won the Cormier trilogy through tactical adjustments between fights.

## Legacy

Stipe Miocic holds the record for the most consecutive UFC Heavyweight Championship defenses (3). He defeated the most decorated opponents of any heavyweight champion: Fabricio Werdum, Alistair Overeem, Junior Dos Santos, Francis Ngannou, and Daniel Cormier. He is universally acknowledged as the greatest UFC Heavyweight Champion of all time.
