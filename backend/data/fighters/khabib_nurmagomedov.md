# Khabib Nurmagomedov — Undefeated Lightweight Champion

## Personal Information

- **Full Name**: Khabib Abdulmanapovich Nurmagomedov
- **Nickname**: The Eagle
- **Born**: September 20, 1988, Sildi, Dagestan, Russian SFSR (now Russia)
- **Nationality**: Russian (Avar/Dagestani)
- **Height**: 5 ft 10 in (178 cm)
- **Reach**: 70 inches (178 cm)
- **Fighting Style**: Sambo, Wrestling, Judo

## Fighting Record

Khabib Nurmagomedov retired with a perfect professional MMA record of 29 wins, 0 losses — one of only a handful of fighters to retire undefeated with a championship pedigree.

- **Wins**: 8 by knockout/TKO, 11 by submission, 10 by decision
- **Losses**: 0

## Championship History

**UFC Lightweight Championship (155 lbs)**: Won on April 7, 2018 at UFC 223, defeating Al Iaquinta by unanimous decision after the originally scheduled champion Tony Ferguson was forced to withdraw due to injury. Khabib defended the title three times before retiring.

## Title Defenses

1. **UFC 229 (2018)**: Defeated Conor McGregor by rear-naked choke in round 4.
2. **UFC 242 (2019)**: Defeated Dustin Poirier by rear-naked choke in round 3.
3. **UFC 254 (2020)**: Defeated Justin Gaethje by triangle choke in round 2 — his final fight.

## Fighting Style & Attributes

Khabib Nurmagomedov is widely considered the greatest grappler in MMA history. His father Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov trained him in Sambo and wrestling from childhood in the mountains of Dagestan.

- **Takedowns**: Khabib's level changes and double-leg takedowns are virtually unstoppable. He has a 90%+ takedown accuracy rate in the UFC.
- **Top Control**: Once on the ground, his pressure and body positioning make escape almost impossible. Opponents describe feeling like "a bear is lying on top of them."
- **Ground-and-Pound**: Relentless striking from dominant positions wears opponents down systematically.
- **Fence Work**: Expert at pinning opponents against the cage and using it to drag them to the ground.
- **Submission Game**: 11 submission victories in his career, including trademark rear-naked chokes and triangle chokes.
- **Durability**: Never knocked down or submitted in his entire career.

## Father Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov

Khabib's father, Abdulmanap, was his trainer, coach, and the architect of his entire career. Abdulmanap was a Honored Coach of Russia in Sambo and Judo and held the rank of Colonel in the Russian army. He developed the training methodology — daily sessions in the mountains, wrestling with bears as children — that shaped Khabib and dozens of other elite Dagestani fighters.

Abdulmanap Nurmagomedov died on July 3, 2020 due to COVID-19 complications. After his passing, Khabib fought one more time at UFC 254 — submitting Justin Gaethje in round 2 — before announcing his retirement, dedicating the win to his father.

## The Conor McGregor Fight — UFC 229

UFC 229 on October 6, 2018, generated 2.4 million PPV buys in North America, making it the highest-selling UFC pay-per-view of all time at that point. Khabib dominated McGregor with relentless takedowns and pressure before sinking in a neck crank/rear-naked choke combination in round 4.

After the fight, both camps engaged in a brawl: Khabib leapt over the cage to attack McGregor's corner member, while McGregor was attacked inside the octagon. Both men were fined $500,000 and received temporary suspensions.

## Training Camp — AKA & Dagestan

Khabib trained primarily at American Kickboxing Academy (AKA) in San Jose, California, alongside teammates Luke Rockhold, Cain Velasquez, Daniel Cormier, and Robert Whittaker. He also maintained a rigorous training base in Dagestan.

His training camps in Dagestan became famous for their altitude (mountainous terrain), extreme physical demands, and the sheer depth of wrestling talent.

## Legacy & Retirement

Khabib retired on October 24, 2020, after his father's passing and the defeat of Justin Gaethje. He finished his career ranked #1 pound-for-pound in the world. His 13-0 record in UFC title fights is the best undefeated title-fight record in promotion history.

He is frequently cited alongside Jon Jones in discussions of the greatest MMA fighter ever. His dominance — winning every round of every fight in the UFC — is unparalleled. He has since moved into management and promotion, founding Eagle FC.
