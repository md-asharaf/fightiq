# UFC Records and Milestones

## Championship Records

### Most UFC Title Defenses (Overall)
1. **Anderson Silva** — 10 consecutive Middleweight title defenses (2006–2012)
2. **Jon Jones** — 11 Light Heavyweight title defenses (2011–2019, across two separate reigns)
3. **Demetrious Johnson** — 11 consecutive Flyweight title defenses (2012–2018) — UFC record for most consecutive defenses by any champion

### Most Title Defenses (By Division)
- **Heavyweight**: Stipe Miocic — 3 consecutive defenses
- **Light Heavyweight**: Jon Jones — 11 total (across 2 reigns)
- **Middleweight**: Anderson Silva — 10 consecutive
- **Welterweight**: Kamaru Usman — 5 consecutive
- **Lightweight**: Khabib Nurmagomedov — 3 (retired undefeated)
- **Featherweight**: José Aldo — 7+ consecutive
- **Bantamweight**: Dominick Cruz — multiple defenses across two reigns
- **Flyweight**: Demetrious Johnson — 11 consecutive (set overall UFC record)
- **Strawweight (Women's)**: Joanna Jedrzejczyk — 6 consecutive
- **Flyweight (Women's)**: Valentina Shevchenko — 7 consecutive
- **Bantamweight (Women's)**: Amanda Nunes — 7 consecutive

### Two-Division Champions (Simultaneous)
The following fighters have held UFC titles in two different weight classes:
1. **Randy Couture** — Heavyweight and Light Heavyweight (not simultaneously)
2. **BJ Penn** — Welterweight and Lightweight (not simultaneously)
3. **Georges St-Pierre** — Welterweight and Middleweight (consecutive)
4. **Conor McGregor** — Featherweight (145) and Lightweight (155) — simultaneously (2016)
5. **Daniel Cormier** — Light Heavyweight and Heavyweight — simultaneously (2018)
6. **Amanda Nunes** — Women's Bantamweight (135) and Featherweight (145) — simultaneously (2018)
7. **Henry Cejudo** — Flyweight and Bantamweight — simultaneously (2019)
8. **Israel Adesanya** — Attempted LHW but failed; Middleweight champion only
9. **Alex Pereira** — Middleweight (briefly, 2022-2023), then Light Heavyweight (2023–)
10. **Jon Jones** — Light Heavyweight and Heavyweight (two separate reigns)

## Finishing Records

### Most Knockouts/TKOs in UFC History
1. **Derrick Lewis** — 13+ KO/TKO finishes (most in UFC history)
2. **Vitor Belfort** — 12 KO/TKOs
3. **Anderson Silva** — 12+ KO/TKOs

### Most Submissions in UFC History
1. **Charles Oliveira** — 21 submission victories (all-time UFC record)
2. **Royce Gracie** — 11 submission victories
3. **Demian Maia** — 11 submission victories

### Fastest KO in UFC History
- **Jorge Masvidal** vs. Ben Askren at UFC 239 (2019): KO via flying knee in 5 seconds — the fastest KO in UFC history

### Fastest Submission in UFC History
- **Ronda Rousey** vs. Alexis Davis at UFC 175 (2014): Arm-bar in 16 seconds

### Fastest Finish in UFC Title Fight History
- **Conor McGregor** vs. José Aldo at UFC 194 (2015): KO in 13 seconds

## The Anderson Silva Era — Most Dominant Reign

Anderson Silva's middleweight championship reign from 2006-2012 is considered the most dominant in UFC history:
- 2,457 days as champion
- 10 consecutive title defenses
- Finished opponents via KO, TKO, submission, and decision
- Victories over Rich Franklin (twice), Dan Henderson, Chael Sonnen (twice), Forrest Griffin, Vitor Belfort, and others
- First UFC champion from Brazil to achieve such dominance

## The Georges St-Pierre Era

Georges St-Pierre's welterweight reign:
- Two separate reigns as Welterweight Champion
- 9 title defenses total (most in welterweight history)
- Won championships at 170 and 185 lbs
- Known for impeccable wrestling and game-planning
- Only champion to return after a 4-year absence and win a title

## Significant Firsts

- **First UFC Event**: November 12, 1993 (UFC 1, Denver, Colorado)
- **First Female UFC Champion**: Ronda Rousey (Bantamweight, 2013)
- **First Women's UFC Fight**: Liz Carmouche vs. Ronda Rousey, UFC 157 (February 2013)
- **First UFC Event at Madison Square Garden**: UFC 205 (November 2016)
- **First Trillion-Dollar Valuation Milestone**: Endeavor Group (UFC parent) IPO in 2021
- **First APEX Center Card**: UFC on ESPN (May 2020, during COVID-19 pandemic)
