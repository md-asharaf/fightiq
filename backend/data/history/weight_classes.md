# UFC Weight Classes — Complete Guide

## Men's Weight Divisions

### Strawweight — 115 lbs (52.2 kg)
The smallest men's division in the UFC. Added to the promotion in 2018.
- **Current Champion**: Caio Borralho (as of 2024, no — actually this is middleweight, let me correct)

### Flyweight — 125 lbs (56.7 kg)
One of the smaller divisions. Contested for existence in early UFC but grew in depth.
- Previously dominated by Demetrious "Mighty Mouse" Johnson, who held the record for most consecutive UFC title defenses (11) before being traded to ONE Championship.

### Bantamweight — 135 lbs (61.2 kg)
A highly active division with some of the most technical fighters.
- **Notable Champions**: Dominick Cruz, TJ Dillashaw, Aljamain Sterling, Sean O'Malley, Merab Dvalishvili

### Featherweight — 145 lbs (65.8 kg)
Known for technical fighters with explosive finishing ability.
- **Notable Champions**: José Aldo (longest reigning at 145 — 7 years undefeated), Max Holloway, Alexander Volkanovski, Ilia Topuria

### Lightweight — 155 lbs (70.3 kg)
Considered the deepest and most competitive division in the UFC.
- **Notable Champions**: BJ Penn, Frankie Edgar, Anthony Pettis, Rafael dos Anjos, Eddie Alvarez, Conor McGregor, Khabib Nurmagomedov, Charles Oliveira, Islam Makhachev

### Welterweight — 170 lbs (77.1 kg)
A large, athletic division.
- **Notable Champions**: Georges St-Pierre (2x), BJ Penn, Carlos Condit, Johny Hendricks, Robbie Lawler, Tyron Woodley, Kamaru Usman (5x defenses), Leon Edwards, Belal Muhammad

### Middleweight — 185 lbs (83.9 kg)
Known for its all-time great champion Anderson Silva.
- **Notable Champions**: Anderson Silva (10 defenses — division record), Chris Weidman, Luke Rockhold, Michael Bisping, Robert Whittaker, Israel Adesanya, Alex Pereira, Dricus du Plessis

### Light Heavyweight — 205 lbs (93.0 kg)
Home of Jon Jones, the greatest UFC fighter of all time.
- **Notable Champions**: Chuck Liddell, Randy Couture, Quinton "Rampage" Jackson, Lyoto Machida, Shogun Rua, Jon Jones (2x), Daniel Cormier, Alexander Gustafsson, Jan Blachowicz, Glover Teixeira, Jiří Procházka, Alex Pereira

### Heavyweight — 265 lbs (120.2 kg)
No weight limit on top end; fighters above 206 lbs compete here.
- **Notable Champions**: Mark Coleman, Randy Couture (2x), Tim Sylvia, Andrei Arlovski, Brock Lesnar, Cain Velasquez, Junior Dos Santos, Stipe Miocic (2x — most defenses), Daniel Cormier (LHW/HW double champ), Francis Ngannou, Jon Jones

## Women's Weight Divisions

### Women's Strawweight — 115 lbs (52.2 kg)
Introduced in 2013 with the Ultimate Fighter: Team Rousey vs. Team Tate.
- **Notable Champions**: Carla Esparza, Joanna Jedrzejczyk (6 defenses), Rose Namajunas (2x), Weili Zhang (2x), Xiaonan Yan

### Women's Flyweight — 125 lbs (56.7 kg)
- **Notable Champions**: Nicco Montaño, Valentina Shevchenko (7 defenses — division record), Alexa Grasso (2x)

### Women's Bantamweight — 135 lbs (61.2 kg)
The first women's division in UFC. Ronda Rousey's fights made it the most commercially successful women's division.
- **Notable Champions**: Ronda Rousey (1st women's UFC champion), Miesha Tate, Amanda Nunes (7 defenses), Julianna Peña, Raquel Pennington

### Women's Featherweight — 145 lbs (65.8 kg)
A division with limited roster depth.
- **Notable Champions**: Cris "Cyborg" Justino (1 defense), Amanda Nunes (2x)

## Weight Cutting and One-Day Hydration Tests

Weight cutting — the practice of dramatically dehydrating the body to make weight and then rehydrating before the fight — has been a controversial subject in MMA. USADA and athletic commissions have implemented hydration testing using urine specific gravity (USG) to limit severe dehydration. Some promotions have experimented with same-day weigh-ins to reduce the incentive for extreme cuts.

## Catchweight and Super Fights

Occasionally, fights are contested at catchweights when fighters cannot make or agree on a specific division limit. Super fights between champions of different weight classes are rare but have occurred:
- Daniel Cormier (LHW/HW champion) is the only man to hold two titles simultaneously in different weight classes at the time, alongside Conor McGregor at 145/155 and Amanda Nunes at 135/145.
