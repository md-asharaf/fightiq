# UFC 229 — Khabib Nurmagomedov vs. Conor McGregor

## Event Information

- **Event**: UFC 229
- **Date**: October 6, 2018
- **Venue**: T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA
- **Attendance**: 20,034
- **PPV Buys**: 2.4 million (North America) — highest in UFC history at the time
- **Card**: Lightweight Championship + 4 other fights

## Main Event: Khabib vs. McGregor

### Context and Lead-up

UFC 229 culminated one of the most bitter rivalries in sports history. The bad blood between Khabib Nurmagomedov and Conor McGregor escalated dramatically in April 2018 when McGregor flew to Brooklyn and attacked the bus carrying Khabib to a UFC media event, throwing a hand truck through the bus window. Multiple fighters were injured. McGregor was criminally charged with assault and eventually pleaded guilty to disorderly conduct.

The fight was the most anticipated in MMA history, with the narrative of an undefeated Muslim grappler from Dagestan versus the world's most famous combat sports personality from Ireland.

### Fight Results

**Winner**: Khabib Nurmagomedov by Rear-Naked Choke, Round 4 (R4:08)

**Round-by-Round Summary**:
- **Round 1**: Khabib established his wrestling early, scoring multiple takedowns. McGregor tried to use footwork but was taken down repeatedly.
- **Round 2**: McGregor landed a good right hand but Khabib continued to drag him down. Khabib applied brutal ground-and-pound.
- **Round 3**: Khabib secured his back during a scramble and locked in a rear-naked choke. McGregor survived by spinning out — one of the few times anyone had escaped Khabib's back control.
- **Round 4**: Khabib got to the back again and this time the choke was locked in tightly. McGregor tapped at 4:08.

### Post-Fight Brawl

Immediately after the fight, Khabib leapt over the octagon cage toward McGregor's corner, attacking the corner member Dillon Danis. Simultaneously, members of Khabib's corner entered the octagon and attacked McGregor. A multi-person brawl ensued in and around the cage.

The brawl resulted in:
- Both fighters withheld their purses initially
- Both fined $500,000
- Khabib suspended 9 months by NSAC
- McGregor suspended 6 months

### Significance

UFC 229 remains the highest-selling UFC pay-per-view of all time in North America as of its date (2.4 million buys). The fight demonstrated that Khabib's wrestling-based system was unstoppable even against McGregor's elite striking. It is considered a landmark event in the globalization of MMA.

## Preliminary and Co-Main Card

- **Co-Main**: Tony Ferguson vs. Anthony Pettis (Lightweight) — Ferguson TKO R1
- Derrick Lewis vs. Alexander Volkov — Lewis KO R3 (comeback of the year)
- Michelle Waterson vs. Felice Herrig

## Legacy

UFC 229 is considered the most significant event in UFC history by multiple metrics: attendance, PPV buys, cultural impact, and the quality of the main event itself. Khabib's performance cemented him as the greatest lightweight of all time.
