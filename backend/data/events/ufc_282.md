# UFC 282 — Blachowicz vs. Ankalaev

## Event Information

- **Event**: UFC 282
- **Date**: December 10, 2022
- **Venue**: T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA
- **Attendance**: ~20,000
- **Significance**: Light Heavyweight Championship + controversial main event result

## Main Event: Jan Blachowicz vs. Magomed Ankalaev (Light Heavyweight Title)

### Context

Jan Blachowicz fought Magomed Ankalaev for the vacant Light Heavyweight Championship. The title was vacated after Jiří Procházka suffered a shoulder injury requiring surgery. Ankalaev entered on an 8-fight unbeaten streak in the UFC and was considered a favorite by many analysts.

### Fight Result

**Result**: SPLIT DRAW (29-28, 28-29, 28-28)

This was one of the most controversial results in recent UFC history. Blachowicz retained his interim claim and Ankalaev was denied the title despite being broadly considered the more active fighter in the later rounds. The draw sparked significant debate about judging standards.

## Notable Fights on the Card

### Paddy Pimblett vs. Jared Gordon (Lightweight)
**Winner**: Paddy Pimblett by unanimous decision

Another highly controversial result. Most media members scored the fight for Jared Gordon, who appeared to land more significant strikes throughout. Pimblett's celebrity status and crowd popularity may have influenced the judges. The result led to widespread calls for judging reform.

### Bryce Mitchell vs. Ilia Topuria (Featherweight)
**Winner**: Ilia Topuria by submission (arm triangle), Round 2

Georgian prospect Ilia Topuria delivered an emphatic performance against the previously undefeated Arkansas wrestler Bryce Mitchell. Topuria's technical grappling and submission skills were on full display.

### Raul Rosas Jr. (18 years old) — UFC debut
UFC's youngest signed fighter made his debut on the prelims, winning by submission.

## Significance

UFC 282 is primarily remembered for two controversial decisions and the frustrating inconclusive main event. It sparked renewed public debate about MMA judging and the need for reform in athletic commission scoring methodology.
