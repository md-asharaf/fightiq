# UFC 300 — Pereira vs. Hill

## Event Information

- **Event**: UFC 300
- **Date**: April 13, 2024
- **Venue**: T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA
- **Attendance**: 20,000+ (sold out)
- **Significance**: The 300th numbered UFC pay-per-view event — a historic milestone

## Main Event: Alex Pereira vs. Jamahal Hill

### Context

Alex Pereira defended his UFC Light Heavyweight Championship against former champion Jamahal Hill. Hill had vacated the title due to injury in 2023. Pereira had won the title at UFC 295.

### Fight Result

**Winner**: Alex Pereira (C) by KO, Round 1 (R1:4:07)

Pereira landed a devastating short right hand that dropped Hill, then finished with follow-up ground strikes. The finish was so swift and brutal that it immediately became one of the most-viewed KO highlights of 2024.

## Co-Main Events

### Bo Nickal vs. Cody Brundage (Middleweight)
- **Winner**: Bo Nickal by submission (RNC), Round 1

### Max Holloway vs. Justin Gaethje (Lightweight Title Fight - BMF Title)
**Winner**: Max Holloway by KO, Round 5, Clock: 4:59 (final second of the fight!)

This was universally declared one of the greatest finishes in UFC history. Holloway and Gaethje agreed to stand and trade in the final 30 seconds. Gaethje rocked Holloway, but with 1 second left on the clock, Holloway landed a straight left hand that KO'd Gaethje before the buzzer. The crowd erupted in what many called the most dramatic single moment in UFC history.

### Charles Oliveira vs. Arman Tsarukyan (Lightweight)
**Winner**: Charles Oliveira by unanimous decision (5 rounds)

A tactical, high-level lightweight battle over 5 rounds.

### Jiri Prochazka vs. Aleksandar Rakic (Light Heavyweight)
**Winner**: Jiri Prochazka by TKO, Round 3

### Zhang Weili vs. Yan Xiaonan (Strawweight Championship)
**Winner**: Zhang Weili (C) by TKO, Round 3

### Kayla Harrison vs. Holly Holm (Bantamweight)
**Winner**: Kayla Harrison by unanimous decision

Harrison's UFC debut was dominant against the former champion.

## Significance and Legacy

UFC 300 was the most star-studded card in UFC history and is frequently cited as the greatest single event the promotion has ever produced. The Holloway vs. Gaethje finish at the final second ensured it will be remembered forever. Alex Pereira's dominant title defense further cemented him as the most dangerous fighter in combat sports. The event set records for digital streaming viewership.

## Records Set at UFC 300

- One of the most-watched UFC events globally
- Multiple knockouts of the year candidates from a single card
- Highest volume of title and championship fights on a single card in UFC history
