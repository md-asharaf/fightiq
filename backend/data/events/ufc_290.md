# UFC 290 — Volkanovski vs. Rodriguez

## Event Information

- **Event**: UFC 290
- **Date**: July 8, 2023
- **Venue**: T-Mobile Arena, Las Vegas, Nevada, USA
- **Attendance**: ~20,000
- **Significance**: Dual championship fights on the main card

## Main Event: Alexander Volkanovski vs. Yair Rodriguez (Featherweight Championship)

### Context

Alexander Volkanovski defended his Featherweight Championship against Yair "El Pantera" Rodriguez. Volkanovski was coming off a back-to-back matchup at Lightweight with Islam Makhachev (UFC 284 decision loss, UFC 294 KO loss). He returned to Featherweight to defend his title.

### Fight Result

**Winner**: Alexander Volkanovski (C) by TKO, Round 3

Volkanovski controlled the fight with elite wrestling and ground-and-pound. In round 3, he landed a series of punches on a grounded Rodriguez that forced the stoppage. It was a dominant performance that reaffirmed his featherweight supremacy.

## Co-Main Event: Brandon Moreno vs. Alexandre Pantoja (Flyweight Championship)

### Context

Brandon Moreno made his third defense of the flyweight title against Alexandre Pantoja — a rematch of their TUF 24 finale fight from 2016.

### Fight Result

**Winner**: Alexandre Pantoja by submission (rear-naked choke), Round 4

Pantoja captured the Flyweight Championship in a back-and-forth war. Moreno was nearly finishing the fight at various points, but Pantoja's toughness and submission game proved decisive.

## Notable Fights

### Robbie Lawler vs. Niko Price (Welterweight)
**Winner**: Robbie Lawler by TKO in Round 1
Lawler's retirement fight ended with an emphatic finish.

### Rose Namajunas vs. Amanda Ribas (Women's Strawweight)
A highly technical striking showcase.

## Significance

UFC 290 was one of the stronger cards of 2023, delivering two championship finishes and building toward the featherweight and flyweight title pictures for the second half of the year. Pantoja's title win began a dominant reign. Volkanovski's defense affirmed he remained the greatest featherweight of his era despite his weight-class-jumping losses.
